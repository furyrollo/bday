Concept: “The Birthday Feast”
She opens a sealed letter, walks through the feast, and ends on your personal message.

Page flow

Wax-sealed envelope. Tap to break the seal. Falling autumn leaves and floating candles in the background.
Birthday letter. A parchment note in the style of a Hogwarts acceptance letter: “Dear [name], we are delighted to inform you that you have been accepted into another year of being amazing.”
The Feast (food). A menu of cards with pun names for her actual favourite dishes, plus butterbeer, pumpkin pasties, treacle tart. Optional: a “pick your dessert” button.
Potions Class (makeup). A “Which spooky look are you?” quiz, sorting-hat style, where every result is a makeup look with potion-style shade names.
Collectible cards. Chocolate-frog style cards, each one a reason you appreciate her. Tap to flip and reveal.
Final page. Your voice note or personal message, with a big “Happy Birthday” and a confetti of bats and pumpkins.

Design direction

Colors: deep plum/near black, burnt orange, antique gold, parchment cream.
Fonts: a gothic serif for headings (Cinzel or IM Fell), a clean readable font for body text.
Touches: wand cursor with a sparkle trail, flickering candle glow, small hidden easter eggs (tap the pumpkin, the owl, the moon).
Mobile first. She will almost certainly open it on her phone.

Practical ideas

Lock it until midnight on the 31st with a countdown (“The feast begins in...”). It builds anticipation and fits the 3 day timeline.
Keep it private: unlisted link, noindex tag, no full names on the page.
Skip official Harry Potter art and logos. Use CSS, emoji, and your own illustrations so it feels homemade and stays safe to host.
